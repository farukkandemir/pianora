# Pricing research and first pricing model

14 Sep 2026. Research done before any payment code exists. Read this before touching subscriptions, paywalls or in-app purchase.

## Decision so far

The owner has decided on a subscription model. Free versus paid, prices and placement below are the recommendation from this research and are **not yet decided by the owner**.

## What the industry data says (RevenueCat and Adapty 2025 and 2026 reports; vendor data, directional)

- Weekly plans: about 3 % of subscribers still paying after 12 months, against 17 % monthly and 44 % annual. Highest refund rates. Annual has the biggest first-renewal cliff; survivors stay.
- Education is an annual category. Weekly dominates games and utilities.
- Freemium to paid converts about 2.6 % of a large pool; a no-card trial about 18 %; a card-required trial about 49 % of those who start, but roughly 94 % of installs never start any trial.
- Trial length barely matters past five days (under 4 days about 26 %, 5 to 9 days about 45 %, longer no better). Activation matters more than days.
- Conversion happens in the first session, after a value moment, not before.
- Price medians across all apps: about $13 a month, $38 a year. RevenueCat suggests $99+ a year for Education, but that is for curricula.
- Apple: one introductory offer per person per subscription group (hence trials attach to yearly); Guideline 3.1.2 requires price, duration and auto-renewal terms on the paywall itself plus links to terms and privacy; Restore purchases is required; a subscription must deliver "ongoing value" or review rejects it.

## Recommendation

**Gate the library, not the features.**

Free forever: two pieces in the library at a time (imported or catalogue), with everything working on them (Wait Mode, loop, hands, on-screen keyboard, MIDI, Listen); the seven beginner catalogue pieces always available to add.

Paid: unlimited pieces, the intermediate and advanced catalogue, painted covers for imported pieces (the one per-use cost).

Reasoning: the free tier is the real product on a small library, so the person paying has already practised. Nothing a free user used is taken away. Feature gating is what produces "note checker" reviews. Ongoing library growth and server-painted covers satisfy Apple's "ongoing value".

**Three prices, no weekly.**

| Plan | Price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $5.99 | for one piece before a recital |
| Yearly (default) | $34.99 | $2.92 a month, 51 % off; on the industry median |
| Lifetime | $69.99 | two years of yearly; the Synthesia/forScore buyer |

Family Sharing on.

**Trial:** seven days free on yearly only, as Apple's introductory offer. Price and renewal date in plain words above the button. Never before the user has practised.

**Paywall placement:** when a free user adds a third piece (Browse, Add music, or first-piece on a reinstall); when a free user taps an intermediate or advanced catalogue piece; a quiet row in Settings with Restore purchases. Never in onboarding.

**Implementation:** RevenueCat with `react-native-purchases` (free until $2,500 a month revenue, then 1 %). Handles receipts, entitlements, trials, restore, dashboard. Needs a config plugin and a native rebuild. `react-native-iap` was archived April 2026; `expo-iap` is its successor but leaves receipt validation and subscription state to the app. Direct StoreKit in a native module is possible but not worth it for a first app.

**Before any code (owner's accounts, not code):** Paid Apps agreement, banking and tax in App Store Connect; a public privacy policy and terms page; the three products in one subscription group with the trial on yearly; a sandbox tester account.

**Changeable later without an app update:** prices, trial length, the free cap. Existing subscribers can keep their price. Watch trial starts per install and trial to paid; move prices, not the model.
